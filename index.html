About Jobs and Interns

Jobs and Interns is Sri Lanka's most trusted platform connecting talented university undergraduates with verified employment opportunities. We've built a thriving community of over 59,000 followers and 12,000+ active members across 12+ WhatsApp communities, representing both state and non-state universities nationwide.

Our network spans 50+ partner companies across Sri Lanka and internationally, delivering verified paid job opportunities daily—all without any sharing fees or hidden costs.

Whether you're a student seeking your next career move or an HR professional looking to discover exceptional talent quickly, Jobs and Interns is your ultimate recruitment solution.
